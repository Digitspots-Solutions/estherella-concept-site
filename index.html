About Rebecca Becky Trust Company Limited

Rebecca Becky Trust Company Limited is a premier wealth management and estate planning firm serving discerning clients across Nigeria. Based in Ogun State with deep roots in the financial services sector, we specialize in comprehensive estate planning, trust administration, wealth preservation, and succession planning.

Our approach combines global best practices with intimate understanding of Nigerian legal frameworks, family dynamics, and cultural values. We recognize that wealth is more than numbers—it represents your life's work, your family's future, and your lasting impact.

Led by experienced wealth management professionals, our firm provides confidential, personalized service tailored to each client's unique circumstances. Whether you're building your first estate plan, restructuring family holdings, or planning multi-generational wealth transfer, we deliver solutions that protect what matters most.

We serve business owners, professionals, families, and institutions who value strategic thinking, meticulous attention to detail, and unwavering commitment to their financial security.

Comprehensive Wealth Management & Estate Planning Services

Estate Planning & Will Preparation

Structured documentation of your wishes, asset distribution strategies, and guardian appointments. We ensure your estate plan reflects your values and protects your beneficiaries while minimizing potential disputes and tax implications.

Trust Administration & Management

Professional establishment and ongoing management of family trusts, charitable trusts, and special purpose trusts. We handle all administrative responsibilities, compliance requirements, and beneficiary communications with absolute discretion.

Succession Planning for Business Owners

Comprehensive strategies for seamless business transition to next generation or designated successors. We address ownership transfer, management continuity, tax efficiency, and family harmony throughout the succession process.

Wealth Preservation Strategies

Proactive asset protection planning, investment oversight, and risk management tailored to preserve and grow your wealth across market cycles and generational transitions.

Retirement Planning

Personalized retirement income strategies, pension optimization, and lifestyle planning to ensure financial independence and dignity in your golden years.

Family Wealth Education

Preparing next generations for responsible wealth stewardship through education, mentorship, and graduated responsibility frameworks.

Legal & Tax Advisory Coordination

Collaboration with your legal and tax professionals to ensure integrated, optimized estate and wealth management solutions that comply with all Nigerian regulations.
